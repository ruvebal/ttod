# Assignment — Team 3, Task 9: Formally review one PR outside your own module

**Seam:** oracle · **Team:** 3 · **Task:** 9 of ~10
**Area(s):** Cross-module · **Verb served:** contribute

Team 3 owns the Oracle Terminal seam, so this task requires you to step outside the `src/components/oracle/` directory and formally review a Pull Request from another team (e.g., Content, Graph, or Accounts). You must use that other module's specific `ASSIGNMENT.md` as your review checklist, verifying that their implementation satisfies their own defined constraints and acceptance criteria, rather than applying your own preferences or the Oracle module's standards.

See the shared explanation: docs/public/teaching/tasks.md#the-three-recurring-tasks

> "The honest AI engineer does not lie to herself about her tools. She knows what they can and cannot do, and she designs her workflow to amplify their strengths."
> — TTOD `arch-037`, *DevIAC Tao (§22)*

Reviewing another module’s code requires the same honesty: you must recognize the boundaries of your own expertise and rely on the target module’s documented standards to judge its success, rather than assuming your own context applies universally.
