# Assignment — Team 4, Task 9: Formally review one PR outside your own module

**Seam:** offline · **Team:** 4 · **Task:** 9 of ~10
**Area(s):** PWA/Offline · **Verb served:** contribute

For Team 4, this task means stepping out of the PWA/Offline boundary to review a pull request from another team (e.g., Content, Graph, Oracle, or Accounts). You must use that specific module's `ASSIGNMENT.md` as your review checklist, verifying that the proposed changes adhere to the constraints, learning outcomes, and acceptance criteria defined for that module, rather than applying PWA-specific heuristics to unrelated code.

See the shared explanation: docs/public/teaching/tasks.md#the-three-recurring-tasks

> "The module that knows its boundaries serves the whole. The module that knows no boundaries becomes the whole—and collapses under its own weight."
> — TTOD `arch-001`, *architecture*

Reviewing another module's work against its own defined boundaries ensures that the PWA team contributes to the system's integrity without overstepping into responsibilities that belong to other teams.
