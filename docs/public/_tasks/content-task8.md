# Assignment — Team 1, Task 8: Formally review one PR outside your own module

**Seam:** content · **Team:** 1 · **Task:** 8 of ~10
**Area(s):** Content/i18n/Proposals UI · **Verb served:** contribute

As the team owning Content, i18n, and the Proposals UI, this task requires you to select a pull request from another team (e.g., Graph, Oracle, or PWA/Offline) and evaluate it strictly against that team’s own `ASSIGNMENT.md`. You are not reviewing for general code quality or style; you are verifying whether their implementation satisfies the specific Learning outcomes, Constraints, and Acceptance criteria defined in their module brief. This cross-module review forces you to read and internalize the constraints of systems you did not build, ensuring you understand how your Content/i18n work integrates with the broader system boundaries.

See the shared explanation: docs/public/teaching/tasks.md#the-three-recurring-tasks

> "The module that knows its boundaries serves the whole. The module that knows no boundaries becomes the whole—and collapses under its own weight."
> — TTOD `arch-001`, *architecture*

Reviewing another team’s PR against their own brief is the practical exercise of respecting those boundaries; you are checking if their module serves the whole without overstepping into yours.
