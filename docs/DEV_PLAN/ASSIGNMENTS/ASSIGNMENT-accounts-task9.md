# Assignment — Team 5, Task 9: Formally review one PR outside your own module

**Seam:** accounts · **Team:** 5 · **Task:** 9 of ~10
**Area(s):** Accounts/Library/Proposals/Public API · **Verb served:** contribute

For Team 5, this task means stepping out of the `src/auth/` and `src/pages/[locale]/wisdom/` boundaries to review a pull request from another team (e.g., Content, Graph, or Oracle). You must use that other module's specific `ASSIGNMENT.md` as your review checklist, verifying that their implementation respects their own defined constraints and acceptance criteria, rather than applying your own team's standards or assumptions. This ensures you are evaluating the work against its intended scope and quality gates, fostering cross-team understanding and rigorous adherence to module-specific definitions of done.

See the shared explanation: docs/public/teaching/tasks.md#the-three-recurring-tasks

> "The module that knows its boundaries serves the whole. The module that knows no boundaries becomes the whole—and collapses under its own weight."
> — TTOD `arch-001`, *architecture*

Reviewing another team's work through their own boundary definitions reinforces that respecting module limits is not just about isolation, but about enabling the system to function cohesively without any single part absorbing responsibilities it was not designed to hold.
