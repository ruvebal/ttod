# U0 decision — complete teaching skeleton, student-owned depth

**Status:** FROZEN by product-owner direction, 2026-09-07

**Owner:** `@crea-comm.net`

**Implementation authority:** none by this record; Phase U gates implementation

## Decision

The FE II student artifact will eventually contain R3b, R4, R5, R6, and R7 at hello-world depth
inside one complete, locally runnable product. The instructor may provide the minimum working seam
needed to explain each lane before collaboration begins. Students own the design and implementation
depth required to satisfy the assessed acceptance criteria.

This refines the earlier R6 deferral without reversing its pedagogical purpose:

- a minimal R6 browser/local-operation seam may be instructor-provided after TS1 freezes its
  subtraction contract;
- the full PWA behavior, caching policy, offline queue, performance work, CI/CD design, and evidence
  remain student assignments;
- R7 receives representative hello-world tests, while the risk-based strategy and comprehensive
  test/evidence surface remain student work; and
- the same “working seam, open depth” distinction applies to R3b–R5.

The rich feasibility reference remains an instructor artifact outside the student-reachable object
graph. The future teaching baseline must have fresh history and no recoverable rich answers.

## Rationale

An R1/R2/R3a-only handoff makes students infer several architectural boundaries before the
instructor can demonstrate the full system. A feature-complete reference leaks the assessed answer.
The chosen middle is a complete vertical skeleton whose individual organs are intentionally shallow:
students can observe the system before they are asked to extend it.

## Non-authorization

This decision does not itself permit code removal, starter distribution, public deployment, cloud
work, course-repository edits, canonical quote mutation, or research-data collection. Those actions
remain behind Phase U, Phase T, teaching, and ethics gates as applicable.
