道 The Tao of Development (TTOD) is a pedagogical wisdom database for developers walking the path. Its
aphorisms are prompts for reflection and teaching, not independent evidence for technical claims.

## How to read the corpus

Browse wisdom by section, level, or tag. Each quotation keeps its canonical identifier, provenance,
language, and rights attribution visible. Translation is never assumed: a translated aphorism is a
separate governed record linked to its source.

## Governance before convenience

Machines may suggest new wording, but only an identified human reviewer can accept it into the
canonical corpus. Public pages render only active entries cleared by the backend rights gate.
