Entrega 1 · Units 1–7 · mid-term defence

You receive a working path, not a finished answer

In Front-end II, TTOD is Entrega 1: the first assessed product (25%, indicative Week 7). It covers the Astro architectural project for Units 2–6—content collections, mandatory English and Spanish routing, islands, multi-framework integration, testing, and an AI-assisted review workflow—as stated in How to Pass Front-end II.

The mid-term (15%, same window) covers Units 1–7. It is not a second product. It checks that you can use the system declaratively, defend your process, and explain the code you own—Astro through PWA, testing and AI review, and performance.

Together, Units 1–7 are roughly half the semester. Units 8–12 (3D, IoT, capstone) come later and are not this deliverable.

What the baseline gives you

The proposed student baseline will run end to end: localized content reaches an Astro page, selected interactions cross Svelte or React islands, requests follow named contracts, governed quotes return to accessible browser states, and representative tests show where evidence belongs.

Content, graph, Oracle, browser operations, and testing will be present only at hello-world depth. Your assignment is to make those areas useful, resilient, accessible, and explainable through your own design choices.

What you will be able to do

  • Trace rendering, state, data, trust, and test ownership across a real product.
  • Extend localized Astro content and information architecture.
  • Design meaningful Svelte graph and React streaming interactions.
  • Define browser offline behavior and measurable performance choices.
  • Build a risk-based testing strategy rather than chase a test count.
  • Disclose AI assistance and keep a human accept/reject/escalate trail.
  • Explain your contribution through code, process evidence, and mid-term defence of understanding.

What the instructor does not assess here

Container administration and cloud deployment are not theory objectives for Entrega 1. You need only the local commands required to run, inspect, and stop the product. Research participation is separate from teaching and assessment; coursework is not automatically research data.
