# Evidence reporting rule

When a task creates, closes, reviews, or hands off a phase:

- lead with the justified state;
- distinguish observation, decision, inference, and unresolved risk;
- record scope and non-scope, commit/worktree, commands/exits, meaningful warnings, changed files,
  protected-data integrity, and safe resume;
- retain failed attempts that affected the diagnosis or next step;
- never hardcode volatile totals in evergreen instructions;
- never mark `DONE` before the governing exit gate and authorized promotion; and
- never let a report authorize publication, deployment, destructive history changes, or external
  communication by implication.
